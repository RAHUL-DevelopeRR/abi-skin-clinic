Privacy Notice

Last updated: 21 August 2026

ABI Skin & Hair Clinic uses the details you submit to manage appointment requests, contact you about the visit, and operate verified patient reviews.

Information collected

For appointments we collect the patient name, mobile number, selected branch, treatment category, requested date and time, and an optional short note. Please do not submit detailed medical history through the website. The website does not collect patient photographs.

Where it is stored and who can access it

Appointment and review data is stored in the clinic's Cloudflare D1 database. Access is limited to authorised clinic staff through the password-protected doctor dashboard and service providers needed to run the website.

Contact and reviews

The clinic may contact you by phone or WhatsApp about your appointment. A completed patient may receive a secure review invitation. Reviews are published only with consent and after clinic moderation.

Retention and requests

Records are kept only as long as needed for clinic operations, legal obligations, and dispute handling. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact the clinic at abiskinandhairclinic@gmail.com or call +91 75981 26430.

Medical and emergency notice

This website provides scheduling and general information, not a diagnosis or emergency service. For urgent symptoms, contact local emergency services or attend the nearest emergency department.
